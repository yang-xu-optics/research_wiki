# Peter L. McMahon

> Principal investigator of the [[mcmahon-lab-cornell|McMahon Lab]] at Cornell University. Corresponding author on both of the McMahon-group [[programmable-photonic-waveguide]] papers — the **linear** [[2d-programmable-linear-waveguide-mcmahon]] (Nature Physics, Dec 2025) and the **nonlinear** [[2d-programmable-nonlinear-waveguide-mcmahon]] (Nature, Jan 2026).

## Overview

Associate (or full) professor in the School of Applied and Engineering Physics at Cornell, with a joint appointment at the Kavli Institute at Cornell for Nanoscale Science. Research program centers on **physics-based computing** — using photonic, electronic, and quantum hardware to perform machine-learning and combinatorial-optimization tasks more efficiently than digital von-Neumann architectures.

## Notable work / output

- [[2d-programmable-linear-waveguide-mcmahon]] — programmable Δn(x, z) for optical neural networks.
- [[2d-programmable-nonlinear-waveguide-mcmahon]] — programmable χ⁽²⁾(x, z) for nonlinear photonics.
- Earlier work (pre-vault) on physics-aware training, neuromorphic photonics, and Ising-machine / optical-coherent-computing approaches.

## Relationships

- PI of [[mcmahon-lab-cornell]].
- Long-running collaboration with [[ntt-research-pin-labs]] (Sunnyvale) — co-authorships on both 2025/26 programmable-waveguide papers.
- Co-author with [[ryotatsu-yanagimoto]] (lead of nonlinear paper, NTT Research) and [[tatsuhiro-onodera]] (co-first of linear paper, also NTT Research).
- Co-affiliated through Cornell with the Kavli Institute for Nanoscale Science.
