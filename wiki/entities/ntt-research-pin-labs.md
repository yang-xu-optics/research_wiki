# NTT Research Physics & Informatics Laboratories (PIN)

> One of three labs at NTT Research, Inc. in Sunnyvale, CA. Long-running collaborator on the [[mcmahon-lab-cornell|McMahon Lab]]'s programmable-photonics program — both [[2d-programmable-linear-waveguide-mcmahon]] and [[2d-programmable-nonlinear-waveguide-mcmahon]] list PIN co-authors as primary contributors.

> **Vault-owner note:** this is the lab the vault owner (yang.xu@ntt-research.com) is affiliated with. Treat ingested papers as potentially internally relevant.

## Overview

NTT Research is the US R&D arm of NTT, headquartered in Sunnyvale. The Physics & Informatics Laboratories (often abbreviated **PIN** or **PHI Labs**) focuses on physics-based and unconventional computing — coherent Ising machines, optical neural networks, programmable photonics, quantum-light sources. Recent output is co-authored heavily with academic partners, especially Cornell and Stanford.
