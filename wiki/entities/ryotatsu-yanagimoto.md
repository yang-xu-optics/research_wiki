# Ryotatsu Yanagimoto

> Lead author of [[2d-programmable-nonlinear-waveguide-mcmahon]] (Nature, Jan 2026). Affiliated with both [[mcmahon-lab-cornell|McMahon Lab at Cornell]] and [[ntt-research-pin-labs|NTT Research PIN Labs]]; corresponding author (`ryotatsu.yanagimoto@ntt-research.com`).

## Overview

Works at the intersection of nonlinear and quantum photonics. Drove the design and demonstration of the programmable χ⁽²⁾ waveguide; co-author on the companion linear refractive-index paper.

## Notable work / output

- First-author: [[2d-programmable-nonlinear-waveguide-mcmahon]] — programmable on-chip nonlinear photonics.
- Co-author: [[2d-programmable-linear-waveguide-mcmahon]] — programmable refractive-index waveguide.
