# Tatsuhiro Onodera

> Co-first author of [[2d-programmable-linear-waveguide-mcmahon]] (Nature Physics, Dec 2025). Co-author of [[2d-programmable-nonlinear-waveguide-mcmahon]]. Affiliated with both [[mcmahon-lab-cornell|McMahon Lab]] (Cornell) and [[ntt-research-pin-labs|NTT Research PIN Labs]].

## Overview

Works on programmable photonic hardware for machine learning and physics-based computing. Co-first author with Martin M. Stein on the 2D-programmable Δn waveguide.
