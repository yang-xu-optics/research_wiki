# Zhaohui Ma

> Zhaohui Ma is first author of the Si3N4-on-sapphire wide-band photonics paper.

## Overview

In this vault, Ma is currently represented through the 2026 paper on direct growth of crack-free stoichiometric [[silicon-nitride]] on sapphire. The affiliations listed in the paper are [[nist]] and the [[joint-quantum-institute]].
