# Integrated quantum photonics

> Integrated quantum photonics builds chip-scale sources, circuits, filters, interferometers, modulators, and detectors for generating and manipulating quantum states of light.

## Origin

The AlGaAs thread enters through waveguide photon-pair generation — [[iii-v-chip-correlated-photon-pair-source-sarrafi]], ultrabright entangled-pair microrings — [[algaas-oi-entangled-photon-pair-microring-steiner]], and the broader AlGaAs quantum-photonics review — [[nonlinear-integrated-quantum-photonics-algaas-baboux]].

## How it works

Quantum photonic chips need high-quality photon-pair or single-photon sources, low-loss routing, stable interferometers, filtering, switching/modulation, and detection. AlGaAs is attractive because one material family can provide strong spontaneous nonlinear generation and possible active/EO integration.

## Trade-offs / Contrasts with

- vs silicon quantum photonics — silicon has mature routing/foundry infrastructure but weaker nonlinear trade-offs and nonlinear absorption issues at telecom.
- vs [[silicon-nitride]] quantum photonics — SiN offers very low loss; AlGaAs offers much stronger nonlinearity and possible active integration.
- vs bulk SPDC sources — integrated sources are smaller and more stable, but must match brightness, purity, indistinguishability, and loss.

## Open questions

- Can AlGaAs integrate source, filtering, modulation, and detection without hybrid complexity dominating?
- Can comb/data-communications infrastructure become useful for quantum source multiplexing and control?
