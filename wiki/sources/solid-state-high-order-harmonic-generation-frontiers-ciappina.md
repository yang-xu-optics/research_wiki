# Solid-state high-order harmonic generation: emerging frontiers in ultrafast and quantum light science

> Review-style synthesis of solid-state HHG as a bridge between ultrafast spectroscopy, quantum optics, topology, excitons, and nanostructured platforms.

## TL;DR

- Surveys Berry-phase, topological, excitonic, lattice, and quantum-light effects in solid-state HHG.
- Frames nanostructured and hybrid plasmonic-semiconductor systems as routes to enhanced and tunable HHG.
- Positions solid-state HHG as a meeting point for material science, attosecond control, and quantum-engineered light.

## Key claims

- Solid-state HHG is no longer just "HHG in a different medium"; it is becoming a spectroscopy of quantum material structure and coherence.
- Quantum and squeezed drivers expand the HHG control space beyond classical waveform engineering.
