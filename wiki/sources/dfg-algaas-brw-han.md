# Difference-frequency generation in AlGaAs Bragg reflection waveguides

> Demonstrates type-II DFG around 1550 nm in AlGaAs BRWs, extending the BRW phase-matching idea beyond SHG.

## TL;DR

- Uses a pump around 778 nm and telecom C-band signal to generate a difference-frequency output around telecom wavelengths.
- Reports DFG across a 40 nm signal span with no observed bandwidth limit within the instrumentation range.
- Frames BRWs as candidates for monolithic parametric devices and integrated photon-pair sources.

## Key Claims

- The same [[bragg-reflection-waveguide|BRW]] phase-matching architecture can support multiple chi(2) processes.
- DFG is strategically important because it points toward tunable IR sources and SPDC/quantum-light architectures.
