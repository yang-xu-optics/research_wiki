# Directionally induced quasi-phase matching in homogeneous AlGaAs waveguides

> Demonstrates that waveguide path geometry can induce effective QPM in homogeneous AlGaAs, avoiding domain inversion or intermixed gratings.

## TL;DR

- Fabricates snake-shaped suspended AlGaAs nanowires on a (001) GaAs wafer.
- Uses changing propagation direction to periodically change the effective chi(2) projection.
- Observes SHG spectral splitting controlled by bend radius and spatial oscillation.

## Key Claims

- Phase matching can be encoded in path geometry, not only material patterning.
- This is conceptually close to whispering-gallery-mode chi(2) selection rules, unfolded into a waveguide.
