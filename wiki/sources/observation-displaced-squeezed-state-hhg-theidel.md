# Observation of a displaced squeezed state in high-harmonic generation

> Experimental report of displaced squeezed-state signatures in HHG, supporting the claim that HHG can generate measurable nonclassical light states.

## TL;DR

- Observes displaced squeezed-state behavior associated with high-harmonic generation.
- Connects HHG experiments to measurable quadrature-level quantum optics.
- Strengthens the bridge between attosecond sources and nonclassical-light generation.

## Key claims

- HHG-generated light can be characterized beyond spectra and intensity, using quantum-state language.
- Experimental observation of squeezing-like signatures is becoming feasible in strong-field settings.
