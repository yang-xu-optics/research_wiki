# Efficient frequency comb generation in AlGaAs-on-insulator

> Early demonstration of Kerr frequency-comb generation in [[algaas-on-insulator|AlGaAs-on-insulator]] microresonators.

## TL;DR

- Shows high-Q AlGaAsOI microresonators with optical parametric oscillation at milliwatt pump thresholds.
- Positions AlGaAsOI as a nonlinear platform combining high Kerr nonlinearity, high index contrast, and low telecom nonlinear loss.
- Establishes a bridge from III-V nonlinear materials to chip-scale comb systems.

## Key Claims

- AlGaAs can behave like a "silicon of nonlinear optics" if processing and effective nonlinearity are solved.
- AlGaAsOI addresses the processing/confinement limitations that held back earlier AlGaAs nonlinear devices.
