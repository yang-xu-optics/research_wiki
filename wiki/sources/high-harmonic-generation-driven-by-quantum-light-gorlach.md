# High-harmonic generation driven by quantum light

> Key paper establishing the conceptual and theoretical program for driving HHG with quantum states of light rather than classical coherent pulses.

## TL;DR

- Treats the HHG driver as a quantum optical state with nonclassical statistics.
- Predicts that quantum-light fluctuations can modify electron trajectories and harmonic emission.
- Provides the bridge from "HHG generates quantum light" to "quantum light controls HHG."

## Key claims

- The driver state's photon statistics are active inputs to strong-field dynamics.
- Quantum fluctuations can become a control resource for harmonic spectra, cutoff, and recollision physics.
