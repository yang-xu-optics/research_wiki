# Symmetry breaking by quantum light in solid-state high-harmonic generation

> Applies quantum-light-induced symmetry breaking to solid-state HHG, where material symmetry and driver statistics jointly shape harmonic emission.

## TL;DR

- Studies solid-state HHG under quantum-light driving conditions that break classical symmetry expectations.
- Connects harmonic selection rules to quantum fluctuations and material symmetry.
- Complements bicircular and structured-light HHG papers by moving the idea into solids.

## Key claims

- Quantum light can act as a symmetry-control resource in solid-state nonlinear optics.
- Solid-state HHG selection rules should be interpreted with both material and field-state symmetry in mind.
