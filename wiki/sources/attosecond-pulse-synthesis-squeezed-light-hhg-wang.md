# Attosecond pulse synthesis from high-order harmonic generation in intense squeezed light

> Proposes that squeezed-light-driven HHG can phase-lock the whole harmonic spectrum, enabling shorter attosecond pulse synthesis.

## TL;DR

- Studies HHG of an atom driven by intense squeezed light.
- Predicts phase locking across the entire HHG spectrum, not only the cutoff region.
- Links synthesized attosecond pulse width to the squeezing parameter.

## Key claims

- Quantum-light statistics can act as a trajectory-control mechanism that improves harmonic phase locking.
- Squeezed-light HHG could become a route to shorter attosecond pulses.
