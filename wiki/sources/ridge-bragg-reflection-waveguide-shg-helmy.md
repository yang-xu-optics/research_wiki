# Second harmonic generation in ridge Bragg reflection waveguides

> Demonstrates phase-matched [[second-harmonic-generation|SHG]] in monolithic AlGaAs ridge [[bragg-reflection-waveguide|Bragg reflection waveguides]] for a 1600 nm pump.

## TL;DR

- Uses AlGaAs ridge BRWs to phase match SHG without relying on natural birefringence.
- Reports 4.5 nm phase-matching bandwidth and peak internal conversion efficiency of 205% W^-1 cm^-2.
- Temperature tuning redshifts the phase-matching peak at about 0.25 nm per degree C.

## Key Claims

- BRWs make semiconductor chi(2) useful by engineering modal dispersion.
- The efficiency is already comparable to periodically poled lithium niobate in normalized terms, suggesting III-Vs can compete when phase matching is solved.
