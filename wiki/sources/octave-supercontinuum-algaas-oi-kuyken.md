# Octave-spanning coherent supercontinuum generation in an AlGaAs-on-insulator waveguide

> Demonstrates octave-spanning coherent supercontinuum generation in a short dispersion-engineered AlGaAsOI waveguide.

## TL;DR

- Generates a supercontinuum spanning roughly 1055-2155 nm in a 3 mm AlGaAsOI nanowaveguide.
- Uses telecom femtosecond pump pulses with 3.6 pJ pulse energy.
- Motivates AlGaAsOI as a single platform for both chi(3) supercontinuum and chi(2) SHG needed in f-to-2f self-referencing.

## Key Claims

- AlGaAsOI's appeal is not only strong chi(3); its intrinsic chi(2) could close the loop for fully integrated self-referenced combs.
- Dispersion engineering is the common language connecting supercontinuum, combs, and broadband nonlinear conversion.
