# Quantum optical signatures of band topology in solid-state high harmonics

> Develops a quantum-optical theory of solid-state HHG aimed at revealing band-topology signatures in harmonic light statistics and correlations.

## TL;DR

- Uses a weak-correlation expansion of photonic and matter degrees of freedom.
- Goes beyond standard Schrodinger-equation HHG treatments by including photonic correlations.
- Connects band topology, matter coherence, and quantum signatures of emitted high harmonics.

## Key claims

- Topological material properties can imprint not only on HHG spectra, but on quantum-optical observables.
- Solid-state HHG may become a quantum-optical probe of band structure.
