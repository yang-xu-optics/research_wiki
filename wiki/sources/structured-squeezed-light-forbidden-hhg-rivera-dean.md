# Structured squeezed light allows for high-harmonic generation in classical forbidden geometries

> Proposes that nonclassical polarization fluctuations can enable HHG in geometries where classical circularly polarized drivers suppress recollision.

## TL;DR

- Studies circularly polarized light with one polarization component squeezed.
- Shows nonclassical fluctuations can restore electron return and enable HHG in a classically forbidden configuration.
- Links spectral differences to modified electron trajectories in the three-step model.

## Key claims

- Quantum fluctuations can break classical HHG selection/recollision constraints.
- Structured quantum light is a control resource, not just a noisy version of classical structured light.
