# AlGaAs nonlinear integrated photonics

> Broad review of AlGaAs nonlinear waveguide platforms, geometries, nonlinear coefficients, and applications in signal processing, combs, and quantum photonics.

## TL;DR

- Reviews AlGaAs as the most developed III-V nonlinear integrated photonics platform.
- Covers chi(2) and chi(3) devices, including wavelength converters, frequency combs, supercontinuum, and quantum photonic circuits.
- Emphasizes the unique III-V promise: passive nonlinear functionality can potentially coexist with active components on the same chip.

## Key Claims

- AlGaAs is not one device concept; it is a platform family with many geometries and fabrication routes.
- The application space links optical communications, all-optical signal processing, frequency combs, sensing, and quantum photonics.
