# Generation of squeezed high-order harmonics

> Develops a formula for the quantum state of high harmonics driven by arbitrary quantum light, with squeezed drivers producing squeezed harmonics.

## TL;DR

- Treats HHG as potential quantum frequency conversion into XUV/UV regimes.
- Shows squeezed coherent driving light can transfer squeezing to high harmonics under suitable timing.
- Predicts richer harmonic quantum states as pump squeezing increases.

## Key claims

- HHG can inherit quantum features from the pump rather than simply destroying them.
- Ionization timing relative to the squeezing phase is a key control variable.
