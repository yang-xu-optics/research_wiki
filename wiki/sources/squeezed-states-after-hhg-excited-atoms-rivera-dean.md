# Squeezed states of light after high-harmonic generation in excited atomic systems

> Studies HHG from initially excited atomic systems as a route to single-mode and two-mode squeezed light, plus heralded kitten states.

## TL;DR

- Predicts significant single-mode squeezing in the driving and low-order harmonic modes.
- Characterizes two-mode squeezing between fundamental and harmonic modes and among harmonics.
- Uses those correlations to propose heralded optical Schrodinger kitten states via photon subtraction.

## Key claims

- Initial matter preparation is a control knob for HHG quantum-state engineering.
- Low-order modes may be especially useful for measurable squeezing and heralding protocols.
