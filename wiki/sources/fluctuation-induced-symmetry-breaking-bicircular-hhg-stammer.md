# Fluctuation-induced symmetry breaking in high harmonic generation for bicircular quantum light

> Studies how quantum fluctuations in bicircular light can break HHG symmetry rules that would hold for classical drivers.

## TL;DR

- Treats bicircular quantum light as an HHG driver with fluctuation-induced symmetry breaking.
- Links emitted harmonic selection rules to quantum fluctuations rather than only classical field symmetry.
- Extends structured/forbidden-geometry HHG ideas into bicircular driving.

## Key claims

- HHG selection rules can become quantum-statistical observables.
- Driver fluctuations may let HHG probe or control symmetry in ways unavailable to deterministic fields.
