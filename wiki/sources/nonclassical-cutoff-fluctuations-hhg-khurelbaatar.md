# Nonclassical cutoff fluctuations in squeezed-light-driven high-harmonic generation

> Proposes HHG cutoff variance as a self-calibrating witness of nonclassical squeezed-light statistics in strong-field observables.

## TL;DR

- Identifies normalized cutoff variance as a sub-standard-quantum-limit witness.
- Emphasizes nanoscale interaction volumes where quantum-field fluctuations are less washed out.
- Frames cutoff fluctuations as an operational route to distinguishing quantum squeezing from classical stochastic noise.

## Key claims

- HHG cutoff behavior can certify nonclassicality of the driver under the right conditions.
- The leading-order scaling can be independent of uncertain effective quantization volume.
