# High-harmonic generation in a crystal driven by quantum light

> Extends quantum-light-driven HHG theory from atoms toward intraband HHG in crystals.

## TL;DR

- Adapts coherent-state/P-distribution quantum-light methods to crystal HHG.
- Studies how nonclassical driving fields affect intraband harmonic generation.
- Provides a conceptual bridge between atomic quantum-light HHG and solid-state quantum HHG.

## Key claims

- The quantum state of the driver matters in crystal HHG, not only in gas-phase recollision physics.
- Solid-state HHG offers a route to quantum-light control in compact material systems.
