# Photon bunching in high-harmonic emission controlled by quantum light

> Experimental approach where bright squeezed vacuum perturbs semiconductor HHG and generates sidebands with super-Poissonian photon statistics.

## TL;DR

- Perturbs ZnO high-harmonic emission with a BSV beam.
- Observes sidebands of the odd-harmonic spectrum with super-Poissonian fluctuations, interpreted as photon bunching.
- Proposes quantum-optical perturbation as a control path toward noncoherent attosecond/XUV states.

## Key claims

- Quantum-light perturbations can be transduced through strong-field electron-hole dynamics into short-wavelength photon statistics.
- Sidebands may be a practical observable for quantum control in semiconductor HHG.
