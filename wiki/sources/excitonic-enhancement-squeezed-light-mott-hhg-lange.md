# Excitonic enhancement of squeezed light in quantum-optical high-harmonic generation from a Mott insulator

> Shows that excitons in a correlated Mott-insulating system can create a distinct squeezed-light response at the exciton energy.

## TL;DR

- Uses an extended Hubbard model to study quantum-optical HHG from a Mott insulator.
- Finds excitons generate a clear squeezing feature at the exciton energy.
- Relates the nonclassical response to time correlations of the current operator.

## Key claims

- Material excitations can localize and enhance HHG nonclassicality in frequency.
- Quantum-optical HHG may become a spectroscopic probe of excitons and correlations, not only a light-source mechanism.
