# Theory of entanglement and measurement in high harmonic generation

> Imports quantum measurement theory into HHG, framing conditioning on harmonic modes as a route to quantum-state engineering of light.

## TL;DR

- Develops measurement operations and POVM language for HHG field modes.
- Treats HHG as a generator of high-dimensional entangled optical states and coherent-state superpositions.
- Helps convert "HHG emits quantum light" into explicit protocols for heralding and conditioning.

## Key claims

- Measurement backaction in HHG is a resource, not only a detection complication.
- Intense laser-matter interactions can be viewed as a quantum technology platform on attosecond time scales.
