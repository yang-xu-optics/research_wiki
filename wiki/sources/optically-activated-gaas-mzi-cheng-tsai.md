# Optically activated integrated optic Mach-Zehnder interferometer on GaAs

> Early demonstration that photoinduced carriers in GaAs waveguides can drive optical phase modulation in an integrated Mach-Zehnder interferometer.

## TL;DR

- Builds a GaAs guided-wave MZI and optically activates one arm to induce phase shift at 1.15 micrometers.
- Reports a pi-radian phase shift with 320 pJ modulating pulse energy over a 22 micrometer interaction length.
- Shows that, for far-below-bandgap IR light, free-carrier index change can dominate free-carrier absorption change.

## Key Claims

- Optical activation can make semiconductor waveguides act as optical-to-optical switches or phase modulators.
- The paper anticipates a broader design pattern: use light or carriers as a reconfigurable control variable inside a photonic material.
