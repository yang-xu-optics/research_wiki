# Femtosecond second-harmonic generation in AlGaAs Bragg reflection waveguides: theory and experiment

> Combines modeling and experiment for femtosecond SHG in AlGaAs BRWs, including group-velocity mismatch, dispersion, two-photon absorption, and self-phase modulation.

## TL;DR

- Uses split-step simulations to assess how chi(3) effects and ultrafast dispersion limit chi(2) SHG efficiency.
- Demonstrates efficient type-II femtosecond SHG in optimized BRWs.
- Reports pump depletion at the phase-matching wavelength, a useful sign that the nonlinear interaction is strong.

## Key Claims

- In semiconductor waveguides, chi(2) and chi(3) are coupled design constraints rather than separable effects.
- BRW phase matching is not only about wavelength matching; group-velocity and dispersion engineering are equally important.
