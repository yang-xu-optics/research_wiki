# Quantum dial for high-harmonic generation

> Proposes using a weak BSV-like quantum field as an optical dial to tune HHG driven by a much weaker classical field, reducing damage while preserving nonlinear control.

## TL;DR

- Studies a weak classical driver perturbed by an even weaker quantum field such as BSV.
- Shows the quantum perturbation can tune nonlinear emission spectra, angular dependence, and ionization.
- Focuses on 2D materials to reduce bulk propagation complications and isolate microscopic dynamics.

## Key claims

- Quantum light can act as a low-energy control knob for HHG spectroscopy.
- A small quantum component may reveal electronic dynamics inaccessible to a purely classical field.
