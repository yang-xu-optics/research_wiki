# Theory of quantum optics and optical coherence in high harmonic generation

> Develops optical-coherence theory for HHG, clarifying how first- and higher-order correlations encode quantum properties of harmonic radiation.

## TL;DR

- Treats HHG through quantum-optical coherence functions.
- Separates spectral/coherence information from photon-statistical and temporal-correlation information.
- Provides the theoretical background for interpreting bunching, antibunching, and mode correlations in HHG.

## Key claims

- HHG's optical coherence must be described at multiple correlation orders, not only via classical spectra.
- Quantum-optical coherence can become a diagnostic of strong-field light-matter correlations.
