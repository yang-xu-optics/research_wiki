# High harmonic generation by bright squeezed vacuum

> Experimental/theoretical milestone showing nonperturbative HHG in solids pumped by macroscopic bright squeezed vacuum.

## TL;DR

- Generates BSV in a single spatiotemporal mode with a very broad photon-number distribution.
- Observes solid-state HHG driven by BSV and finds it can be more efficient than coherent light at the same mean intensity.
- Uses BSV field fluctuations to sample free-carrier dynamics across a broader range of peak intensities.

## Key claims

- Macroscopic quantum light can directly drive strong-field HHG.
- Mean intensity is not enough to predict HHG yield when the driver has nonclassical photon-number fluctuations.
