# Photon anti-bunching in high harmonic generation

> Predicts photon antibunching in HHG, shifting the HHG quantum-optics discussion from ensemble photon statistics toward temporal single-photon correlations.

## TL;DR

- Uses a Heisenberg-picture quantum-optical HHG approach to calculate the intensity correlation function g(2)(tau).
- Predicts antibunching signatures across the harmonic spectrum, a nonclassical temporal-correlation signature without a classical counterpart.
- Distinguishes temporal correlations from photon-number distributions: sub-Poissonian statistics and antibunching are related but not interchangeable.

## Key claims

- HHG photons can carry genuine single-photon-like temporal anticorrelation signatures.
- Measuring g(2)(tau), not just spectra or photon-number variance, could certify a deeper nonclassical aspect of harmonic emission.
