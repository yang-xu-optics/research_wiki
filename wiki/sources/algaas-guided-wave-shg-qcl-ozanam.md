# AlGaAs guided-wave second-harmonic generation at 2.23 micrometers from a quantum cascade laser

> Proof-of-principle frequency doubling of a quantum cascade laser in a GaAs/AlOx waveguide, pointing toward hybrid semiconductor microsystems for difficult wavelength ranges.

## TL;DR

- Demonstrates SHG of QCL light in a multilayer partially oxidized GaAs/AlOx waveguide.
- Uses waveguide-width/form-birefringence design to phase match generation around 2.2-2.4 micrometers.
- Motivates compact coherent sources in the 2-4 micrometer range for spectroscopy and sensing.

## Key Claims

- AlGaAs/GaAs nonlinear waveguides can complement semiconductor lasers by moving light into wavelength ranges where direct sources are weak.
- Hybrid source-plus-converter systems may be more practical than forcing the laser material itself to emit at every target wavelength.
