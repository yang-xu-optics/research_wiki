# Evidence of the quantum optical nature of high-harmonic generation

> Experimental PRX Quantum paper reporting nonclassical photon-statistical signatures and two-mode squeezing in semiconductor HHG.

## TL;DR

- Measures single- and double-beam intensity cross-correlations of harmonic emission from semiconductors.
- Reports nonclassical photon-statistical features, including two-mode squeezing and Cauchy-Schwarz inequality violation.
- Supports the view that HHG can act as a bosonic quantum platform producing nonclassical light, not only a classical XUV/attosecond source.

## Key claims

- Semiconductor HHG can produce measurable nonclassical harmonic radiation.
- The transition from super-Poissonian to Poissonian photon statistics depends on laser intensity.
- Multimode detection theory is needed to interpret the effective squeezing modes in the emitted harmonics.
