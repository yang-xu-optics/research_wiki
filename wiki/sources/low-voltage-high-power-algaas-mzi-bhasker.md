# Low voltage, high optical power handling capable, bulk compound semiconductor Mach-Zehnder modulators

> Manuscript on AlGaAs bulk electro-optic MZMs designed for low Vpi and high optical power handling in analog photonic links.

## TL;DR

- Reports AlGaAs bulk EO MZMs with measured Vpi of about 1 V for a 1 cm electrode.
- Uses a high-bandgap active waveguide region to reduce material and two-photon absorption at 1550 nm.
- Device characteristics remain stable for coupled optical powers approaching 160 mW.

## Key Claims

- AlGaAs modulation can target a different niche than silicon or polymers: low voltage plus high optical power handling.
- Modulators should be evaluated as system components where linearity and optical power handling matter as much as bandwidth.
