# The quantum-optical nature of high harmonic generation

> Foundational paper for treating HHG as a quantum-optical process rather than only a semiclassical attosecond source.

## TL;DR

- Argues that semiclassical HHG misses field-state information in the emitted harmonics and the depleted driver.
- Opens the door to asking about coherence, photon statistics, and quantum correlations of the HHG field modes.
- Serves as an anchor for the later quantum-light-driven and nonclassical-HHG papers in this batch.

## Key claims

- HHG is not merely a classical coherent source at new frequencies; it is a strong nonlinear light-matter interaction that can generate nonclassical optical states.
- The harmonic spectrum is an incomplete observable for the full quantum state of HHG radiation.
