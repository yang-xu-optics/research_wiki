# Edge states and quantum optical high-harmonic generation from topological insulators

> Studies whether topological edge states affect quantum-optical HHG signatures in finite SSH-chain models.

## TL;DR

- Compares quantum-optical HHG from topologically trivial and nontrivial phases.
- Finds squeezing near the band-gap frequency, with topology-dependent differences strongest for smaller systems.
- Attributes differences to overlap between bulk and edge states and dipole coupling strengths.

## Key claims

- Topological edge states can influence HHG nonclassical response, but finite-size and coupling details matter.
- Squeezing is not automatically a universal topological marker; spectra may distinguish phases more robustly at larger sizes.
