# Ultrabright entangled-photon-pair generation from an AlGaAs-on-insulator microring resonator

> Quantum photonics milestone showing ultrabright time-energy entangled photon-pair generation from a low-loss AlGaAsOI microring.

## TL;DR

- Uses a Q > 1 million AlGaAsOI microring resonator for time-energy entangled photon-pair generation near 1550 nm.
- Reports internal generation rate above 20 x 10^9 pairs s^-1 mW^-2, heralded single-photon purity above 99%, and Bell-inequality violation with visibility above 97%.
- Frames AlGaAsOI as a scalable quantum photonics platform because high nonlinearity and potential active integration coexist.

## Key Claims

- AlGaAsOI can overcome the brightness/quality trade-off faced by lower-nonlinearity or higher-loss integrated platforms.
- The same platform used for classical nonlinear optics can produce high-quality quantum states.
