# Continuous-wave quasi-phase-matched waveguide correlated photon pair source on a III-V chip

> Demonstrates correlated photon-pair generation by SPDC in a QPM GaAs/AlGaAs superlattice waveguide under continuous-wave pumping.

## TL;DR

- Reports low-noise correlated photon-pair generation on a III-V chip.
- Achieves a coincidence-to-accidental ratio greater than 100, described as the highest reported in III-V chips at the time.
- Highlights the path to monolithic integration with an on-chip pump laser fabricated on the same superlattice wafer.

## Key Claims

- III-V photon-pair sources are attractive because SPDC can coexist with direct-band-gap pump integration.
- QPM superlattice fabrication is not just a classical frequency-conversion technique; it is a quantum-source enabler.
