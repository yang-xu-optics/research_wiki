# Fabrication of periodically-inverted AlGaAs waveguides for quasi-phase-matched wavelength conversion at 1.55 micrometers

> Fabrication-focused paper on periodically inverted AlGaAs for [[quasi-phase-matching|QPM]] wavelength conversion near telecom wavelengths.

## TL;DR

- Improves periodically inverted AlGaAs fabrication using template planarization by chemical mechanical polishing and low-temperature regrowth.
- Reduces core/cladding corrugation and propagation loss relative to earlier sublattice-reversal epitaxy processes.
- Demonstrates first-order QPM SHG at 1.55 micrometers in a 1.47 mm device, with internal conversion efficiency of 2.2%/W.

## Key Claims

- Periodic inversion gives a direct route to QPM in AlGaAs, but fabrication roughness and regrowth remain serious bottlenecks.
- The paper represents the "hard-code the nonlinear grating into epitaxy" branch of [[semiconductor-phase-matching]].
