# Entanglement and squeezing of the optical field modes in high harmonic generation

> Shows that HHG naturally entangles and squeezes optical field modes once dipole-moment correlations and ground-state depletion are included.

## TL;DR

- Demonstrates that the product-coherent-state picture of HHG relies on approximations that remove dipole correlations.
- Predicts squeezing in individual modes and entanglement between field modes when the atomic ground state is significantly depleted.
- Shows carrier-envelope phase can rotate the squeezed quadrature of the fundamental mode.

## Key claims

- Squeezing and multimode entanglement are intrinsic HHG quantum-optical features, not only effects of exotic input states.
- Ground-state depletion marks the onset of stronger field-mode correlations.
