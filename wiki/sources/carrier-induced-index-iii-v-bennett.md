# Carrier-induced change in refractive index of InP, GaAs, and InGaAsP

> Foundational modeling paper for carrier-induced refractive-index changes in III-V semiconductors, connecting band filling, band-gap shrinkage, and plasma effects to practical phase shifters and switches.

## TL;DR

- Estimates index changes in InP, GaAs, and InGaAsP for carrier concentrations from 10^16 to 10^19 cm^-3 and photon energies from 0.8 to 2.0 eV.
- Predicts index changes as large as about 10^-2 at high carrier concentration, enough to motivate compact [[electro-optic-modulation|phase modulators]] and switches.
- The paper is a bridge between semiconductor device physics and integrated photonics: carrier populations become a knob for optical phase.

## Key Claims

- III-V carrier effects can provide large refractive-index tuning while remaining relevant to low-loss optical devices.
- The useful index response is not one mechanism: band filling, band-gap shrinkage, and free-carrier plasma effects all matter.
- This mechanism later echoes in optically activated GaAs MZIs and in broader ideas about programmable optical media.
