# Nonlinear integrated quantum photonics with AlGaAs

> Review focused on AlGaAs as an integrated quantum-photonics platform combining nonlinear quantum-light generation with circuit functionality.

## TL;DR

- Reviews room-temperature quantum-light sources based on AlGaAs chi(2) and chi(3) nonlinearities.
- Covers circuit components such as polarizing beamsplitters, MZIs, modulators, and tunable filters.
- Frames AlGaAs as a platform whose value comes from combining nonlinear sources, active/EO functions, and possible superconducting-detector compatibility.

## Key Claims

- Integrated quantum photonics is material-pluralistic: no single platform wins every function.
- AlGaAs is compelling because it can unify source brightness, nonlinear strength, EO control, and active integration better than many passive platforms.
