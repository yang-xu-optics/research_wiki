The main differences between these designs are what we suspect the index and conductivity to be.  We know index pretty well (old ellipsometry plots are below), but we don’t know the conductivity quite as well.  We have two pieces with 3um of my annealed oxide left, so we get two more goes at this device.

Ryo observed dep rates of 58.4 nm/min for SRN6 and 67 nm/min for SRN8.  This means we dep SRN6 for 15.5 mins and SRN 8 for 13.5 mins.  We should also do a 10 min heat at 350 just to make sure everything anneals properly at the beginning.  Everything will have 1 min season.  

We know my films deposit at a rate of 62.2 nm/min.  To get 1500 nm, we want to dep for 24 mins.
