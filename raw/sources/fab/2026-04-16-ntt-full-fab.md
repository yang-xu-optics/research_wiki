We will now do second round to get to 17 minute total.  7 more minutes on 1 and 2, 10 more minutes on 1.5.  As for thinning the oxide, I say we leave the same thickness of oxide on top as we have on the bottom.  So this means reducing the etching time a bit for the 1 and 1.5 um wafers.  I still think depositing 3um and thinning is still the best way to make extra sure we have no leakage.  This just gives us higher control, slightly better performance, at no cost other than time (which is immaterial in these matters).

In the past, we use ~10:30 to thin the top cap (this means an etch rate of ~190 nm/min.  So we will etch for ~8 minutes for the 1um thick wafer, ~9 minutes for the 1.5 wafer, and 10:30 for the 2um wafer.  Sounds like a plan!
