# 2024-7-22 further srn dc linear device sweep
The purpose of this document is to do a further sweep of the SRN levels.  Our previous sweep showed SRN 3.5, 4, and 4.5 having too high of a resistance.   SRN 5 seems to be roughly equivalent.  We saw earlier that SRN 6 has great performance, so we definetaly want to do a sweep around there.  We could just try to reproduce the SRN6 and SRN8 results, and they are legitimately interesting.  But, as previous experience shows, reproducibility studies are kinda a waste of time.  Rarely is that the issue.

Lets see if our studies today can do a good sweep.  I hope we can see if the SRN8 and SRN6 results are reasonable with our results from just today.  Lets do 5.5, 6.5, and 7.5.  This should be fairly representitive.  

We know the dep rate of SRN6 is 52.5 nm/min and SRN8 is 66 nm/min.  We want roughly 900 nm of film.  This means we deposity SRN5.5 for ~ 18.5 mins, SRN6.5 for 16.5 mins, and SRN 7.5 for 14.5 mins.  Seems to follow a nice pattern if you ask me.  We will also need to buy a new box and cleave some witness samples.  

Additionally, it would be nice if we could take some frequency data on these chiops and see if they display the frequency dependance that we would like to see.  This means having an oxide reference would be helpful.  I would also like to do a characterization here where we try to see if the chi3 is increased by illumination.  I don’t think all of these chips need oxide.  I would do oxide on SRN6.5.  That means that piece should be a bit larger.  At some level, I don’t mind using up all of our remaining wafer, as I really don’t know what tests we could possibly do after this.  Then again, things always surprise me.  We must season and clean beforehand too.  Lets also buy a bottle
