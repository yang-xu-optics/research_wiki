# 2025-06-11 oxide hard mask development for 2um of sinx
We recently got new SiNx wafers in from SVM.  We will use these wafers to create a proceedure for using an oxide hard mask to etch SiNx.  We are still going to use the ASML as our exposure method.  From our previous calculations, to reliably clear the SiNx, we really need 1.5 um of SiO2.  We are going to use our previous etching recipe (that used CH2F2) to etch the oxide.  This means we will be etching oxide at roughly 160 nm/min.  So we want to etch oxide for roughly 10 mins.  We are told we have a 4:1 etch selectivity, so we need at least 400 nm of resist.  To be safe, we are going to use the 800 nm thick resist recipe (instead of the 600 nm thick resist recipe), as we seem to loose 150 nm through descum.  We will need to up the dose accordingly.
