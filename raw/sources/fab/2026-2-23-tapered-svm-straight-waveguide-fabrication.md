# 2026-2-23 tapered svm straight waveguide fabrication
We are going to fabricate some new tapered waveguids that will hopefully be single mode.  We are going to just do 1 wafer for now, as we are going to get soooo many devices.  Below is the proceedure that we follow.

1. Use ARC and thick resist on gamma.  Expose on ASML using slightly lower dose of 18
2. Descum for 1:20 on 82
3. Etch for 6:40 mins using oxide etch on 100.  Pre clean for 5-10 mins and season for 1 minute
4. 5 mins eco clean + piranha
5. clean 100 for 8 mins, season nitride etch for 2 mins, and etch for 5:15 mins
6. Use piranha, then BOE for 45 seconds.  You can do another piranha after
7. cap with 9+8 smooth oxide, and thin
8. cleave, then RTA at 800 C for 5 mins
9. SRN8 photoconductor deposition

We use the thicker resist recipe too.  We will only need one wafer and it already has oxide.
