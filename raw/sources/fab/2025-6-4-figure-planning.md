# 2025-6-4 figure planning
I want to make some sketches of what the figures we want to make ought to look like so we know what experimental results we are generally going after

Figure 1 (device concept)

We ideally want to give some impression of what the device looks like, the operational principal, and high level idea of the types of things we can do with it

Figure 2 (simple straight waveguides and photonic crystals)

1. Broadband poling on straight waveguides, showing the humps for differnet wavelengths (nice sinc functions would look good too)
2. Poling photonic cyrstals, getting similar plots
3. Perhaps show some SCG stuff by pumping harder

The main point here is we can adapt the poling to different straight-like structures and maintain broadband operation. 1 and 2 are the most important

![Drawing](https://resv2.craft.do/user/preview/c10b6666-b4fd-53a0-9177-1696a144b2d8/doc/D6633F74-F8DD-4934-A8DE-2E6A97A4F0B6/68C1CE8B-534A-42AA-9A06-F16D6F17ABB0_1/0HQPqvG3GKiPcCp4hMQ0fdbuJxdsZDaGv2Czlb3NMswz/Drawing.jpg)

Above is meant to show broad-band behavior and the progamability of the poling period at some specific wavelength.  Easier to show poling period prgammability on straight than spiral, as the polar-caartesian transformation can be a bit annoying

Figure 3 (long distance poling)

Pretty simple.  We want to show poling over a very long distance, preferably for square spirals and circular spirals.  We would show the signal increasing with distance for multiple wavelengths and the transfer functions for those wavelengths.  This should show a narrow-band feature of the device and prove that we can get real interference going

![Drawing](https://resv2.craft.do/user/preview/c10b6666-b4fd-53a0-9177-1696a144b2d8/doc/D6633F74-F8DD-4934-A8DE-2E6A97A4F0B6/22F1EB3A-3C00-459B-B4C0-21840E23CE48_1/gD4quFfgRuduvPV9wX10r6YURVw85Dnad6hj3xftq6Qz/Drawing.jpg)

I might want to plot naive and optimized phase side by side, but the point here is we should these spirals working at a couple of wavelengths (with good scaling and narrow transfer functions), while proving that things would not work if you did not have independent control of the phase

Figure 4 (spectral engineering)

I am a bit less certain about the exact shapes we want, but the effect here is we are sending broadband pulses into our waveguide and then getting unique shapes out.  It would be cool to show some super-continuum-like effects
